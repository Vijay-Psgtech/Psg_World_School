How We Teach

Learning that goes
deeper than content

At PSG World School, learning is not the delivery of information. It is the gradual construction of understanding — through inquiry, reflection, collaboration, and real-world connection. Every lesson is an opportunity to build not just knowledge, but the ability to use it.

  • Inquiry-based learning that puts questions before answers
  • Transdisciplinary units that connect knowledge across subjects
  • Student agency at the centre of every learning experience
  • Assessment that serves growth, not just measurement
  • A bilingual and multilingual environment that values every language
The Whole Child
Academic excellence and personal development are not in competition. We give equal attention to intellectual growth, emotional wellbeing, social development, and physical health.
Inclusion by Design
Every classroom is designed for every learner. Differentiation is not a remedial strategy — it is standard practice. All children deserve access to challenge, support, and belonging.
Purposeful Technology
Technology amplifies great teaching. We use digital tools thoughtfully — to extend inquiry, enhance creativity, and develop the digital citizenship skills children will need for life.
Home–School Partnership
Parents are partners, not customers. We communicate openly, involve families meaningfully, and build the kind of trust that makes a real difference in a child's development.

Grade 5 Where We Are in Place and Time

100 Voices of PSG

A centenary documentary

Grade 5 students produce a documentary film marking PSG & Sons' Charities' hundred years in Coimbatore — told through eight people whose lives were directly changed by PSG institutions. Sarvajana alumni. Rural health clinic patients. Vishnugranthi families. Long-serving hospital staff who remember the institution's earliest years.

The Communications Office must approve the film before it can be screened. The interviewees give final consent. The film is archived as part of the trust's own institutional record. This is not a school project that gets displayed on a wall for a week.

Grade 1
Who We Are

Sensory Stories for Vishnugranthi

Grade 1 students design tactile storybooks for children with Autism and Cerebral Palsy at PSG Vishnugranthi Day Care Centre. The books are tested across three live sessions. The care team gives honest feedback on what actually engages the children and what doesn't. Students revise. The final books are adopted permanently for daily therapeutic use.

Children at Vishnugranthi are the same age as Grade 1 students. The empathy is immediate. The stakes are real.

PSG Vishnugranthi Day Care Centre · Peelamedu
Grade 2
Sharing the Planet

Segregation Champions

Students design a Tamil-English household waste segregation guide tested with 15 families on three Peelamedu streets. The CCMC Sanitation Supervisor for the zone reviews the final version. If it works, he distributes it across the entire zone. Mixed waste collection in Coimbatore has already fallen 40% since 2024 — public participation is the remaining gap.

Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation · Peelamedu Zone
Grade 3
Where We Are in Place and Time

Coimbatore's Living Memories

Grade 3 students interview five elders at Peelamedu care homes who remember Coimbatore from fifty to eighty years ago. The memories are mapped to real city locations — the Noyyal River as a swimming destination, the industrial transformation of Peelamedu, the first generation of PSG students. Each elder confirms their memory before it goes on the map.

Grace Welfare Trust · Shanthi Nilayam · Peelamedu
Grade 4
How We Organise Ourselves

Safe School Zone — Avinashi Road

Grade 4 students redesign the drop-off zone outside PSG World School on Avinashi Road. They collect observation data across five school mornings. Inspector Kavitha of Peelamedu Traffic Police reviews the proposed protocol and provides AI surveillance incident data — Coimbatore installed smart surveillance at 40+ junctions in 2024, which reduced accidents by 12% in monitored zones. The revised system is piloted for two weeks. Before-and-after data is submitted to school leadership for a permanent adoption decision.

Inspector Kavitha · Peelamedu Traffic Police Station
Grade 3
How We Organise Ourselves

Millet for Morning

Students prototype a millet breakfast item for the PSG World School canteen. Recipe tested with 30 students. Reviewed by the dietitian at PSG Hospitals' Dietary Department. Revised. Introduced to the menu. Sales tracked. Energy levels surveyed before and after.

PSG Hospitals Dietary Department · School Canteen

Sharing the Planet

The Noyyal Documentary

A documentary about Coimbatore's most significant river, reduced from 34 tributaries to four since 2003. Students interview elders who swam in it as children. The film is screened at a Siruthuli community event. Audience surveyed before and after.

Siruthuli Environmental NGO · Coimbatore

Why Coimbatore.
Why PSG.

PSG & Sons' Charities — founded in 1926, now operating more than thirty institutions across the city — means PSG World School sits inside one of the most connected philanthropic ecosystems in South India. PSG Hospitals. The Rural Health Training Centre at Vedapatti, serving nearly 26,000 people across fourteen villages. PSG College of Technology. Siruthuli.

These are not field trip destinations. They are partners. The PSG name carries a century of community trust — and when a student walks into a rural health clinic or an environmental NGO office, that trust opens doors.

What parents can expect

01
Questions before answers
Your child will come home talking about the person they interviewed, the constraint they hadn't anticipated, the feedback that required starting a section again.
02
Revision as evidence of learning
Every unit requires at least two full revision cycles. Revision is not a sign that something went wrong — it is the evidence that learning is happening.
03
A real audience at the showcase
Once a year your child presents to an audience that includes the community members who shaped the work — not just the final product, but the drafts that led there.
04
Work that continues after the unit ends
Sensory books adopted by Vishnugranthi. A millet recipe on the canteen menu. A conservation system running in the school. The work does not disappear when the term does.
